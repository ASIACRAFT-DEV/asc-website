The Forge — Seasonal Gear

Every season, the Forge mints a full set of themed gear with passives you level up. Season 1 is SolForge; Season 2 brings Arctis. Here's everything about both.

SolForge is live now and becomes a legacy set once Season 2 begins. Arctis is the new frozen set arriving with the Aug 1 wipe. Jump to a section below.

🛠️ How Seasonal Gear Works

/gear/ascforge upgrade

Forge gear is netherite-equivalent equipment with a twist: every piece carries a named passive ability, and each piece has a Level that you raise to make that passive stronger and more likely to trigger. The whole set shares a look, a currency, and a full-set bonus.

The core loop
  • Every piece has a passive — armor, weapons and tools each do something unique.
  • Level I → VI. Higher levels raise the passive's proc chance. Level VI is Overmax, the peak.
  • Upgrade with the season's shards. Hold a piece and run /ascforge upgrade to spend shards and raise its Level.
  • Infinite durability — Forge gear never breaks.
  • Set bonus. Wear the full armor set for a season-wide bonus that boosts every passive at once.
  • Browse the whole catalog any time with /gear.

Only one seasonal set is craftable at a time — the current season's. When a new season starts, the previous set becomes a legacy set: you keep every piece you own and can still play it, but new keys and shards for it leave the store.

☀️ SolForge — Season 1

Season of the First Sun

Live now — becomes a legacy set on August 1. Our first full seasonal set: sun-touched gear powered by Solar Shards. Charge your Solar Resonance meter as you play to ignite Radiant State, a server-announced power surge. Once Season 2 begins you keep all your SolForge gear and can still use it — but new SolForge Keys leave the store.

Leveling & Overmax
  • Upgrade pieces I → V with Solar Shards via /ascforge upgrade.
  • Push a Level V piece to VI (Overmax) at the Archaeologist using a Seasonal Essence.
  • Every piece has infinite durability.
The SolForge Chest

Spin the seasonal gacha crate for gear and Solar Shards. The grand prize is a guaranteed Level V piece — the only way to mint a max-tier item — backed by a pity system so a streak of bad luck always pays off. SolForge Keys are available in the store while Season 1 lasts, and every dungeon clear drops one too.

🎴 How to Get Forge Gear

  • Season Chest keys — spin the current season's chest (SolForge Chest now, Arctis Chest at launch). Keys are sold in the store and the grand prize is a guaranteed Level V piece.
  • Dungeon clears — every clear drops a seasonal key, so running dungeons steadily builds toward a full set.
  • Shards from gameplay — mining, chopping, hunting and the season's tools all drop shards you spend on /ascforge upgrade.
  • Overmax materials — Seasonal Essence (and its equivalents) push a Level V piece to Overmax at the Archaeologist.
