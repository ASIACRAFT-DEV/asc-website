Season 2 Gameplay Overhaul

Every activity now has a purpose. Progress by actually playing the game — not just farming coins.

❄️ Season 2 is live — Arctis launched August 8. Every system below is finished, installed and beta-tested, with well over a hundred fixes landed off your reports. The Open Beta and its wipe are done; everything you earn from here is the real season. Donator ranks and Season Vault contents were never touched by the wipe.
🖥️ You need the modpack to join. Season 2 runs on a custom interface — our client mod (asc-clientui) ships in the official CobbleAsia modpack and is now required to connect. Grab the pack from Discord before you log in.

Season 2 is all about giving every activity a purpose. In Season 1, we noticed a few unhealthy gameplay loops:

  • Farming money became the best strategy for almost everything.
  • Legendary Pokémon became less valuable because anyone could eventually gamble for them.
  • Battle Pass rewards weren't exciting enough.
  • Battle Tower became repetitive.
  • Some progression systems had too many requirements.

Season 2 aims to fix these problems.

🗺️ One World, One Border

/rtp/spawn/hub

Season 1 sprawled across a handful of overworlds. Season 2 is one map — freshly generated on a brand-new seed, fully pre-generated before you arrive, and fenced in on purpose so the world stays a place instead of an empty frontier.

A finite, fully-loaded map
  • The overworld is capped at a 10,000 × 10,000 square — 5,000 blocks in every direction from spawn
  • Every chunk inside that border is pre-generated, so exploring doesn't lag the server and nobody walks into unloaded terrain
  • A smaller world means you'll actually run into other trainers — bases, shops and clan territory all sit within reach
  • All the themed destinations (Safari, Area Zero, dungeons, event worlds) are still their own separate spaces
/rtp — pick a world, then a biome

Random teleport opens on a world picker, then a biome picker. Two destinations are open: Resource, the pre-generated world you hunt and mine in, and Build, the world you settle in. You can skip straight past either level:

/rtp  ·  /rtp resource cherry_grove  ·  /rtp build  ·  /rtp desert
  • Resource keeps the biome picker — it shows every biome on the map and how much of the world it covers
  • Build is a classic random scatter, so there is no biome list to wait on
  • Every trip is a genuinely random spot — not the one nearest corner everybody else got sent to
  • Oceans and cave-only biomes are excluded, so you never land swimming or buried
  • Cooldowns are counted per world, so a trip out to Resource never locks you out of Build
  • Typing a bare biome name still works exactly as the beta taught you — it targets Resource

Hunting a specific Pokémon? Look up its biome with /pwiki <species>, then /rtp straight there.

A new spawn — and the old hub is still there
  • /spawn drops you at Dragonforstborn, the new Season 2 frozen spawn city — NPCs, shops, warps and the job boards all live here
  • /hub takes you back to the old lobby, kept around for events and nostalgia

💰 Coins & Economy Revamp

Money is no longer the driving force for Legendaries — but coins still matter. In Season 2 they shift from "buy everything" to fuelling the content loop.

What coins are now for
  • Shop purchases, as always
  • Entry cost for the content that rewards Orbs — Battle Tower, Area Zero, Dungeons and Raid Bosses
  • Dungeon Keys are now purchasable with coins — the daily key allowance is removed
  • 15 Orbs a day can still be bought with coins, at 50,000 each
  • Buy Claim Blocks and extra PC slots
  • Convert coins directly into Tokens and Shards

To make that work, we rebalanced the coin economy so coins matter more and money stops being the shortcut to everything.

What was reduced — roughly 60% across the board
  • Coin payouts from Jobs, Fishing, Poké Hunts, the Global Hunt, Area Zero & Raid Bosses, Contracts, Bingo, Daily Login, Playtime and the Solar Pass
  • Donator kit coin payouts were scaled to match — the keys, Cores, candy and balls in every paid kit are untouched, because that's what you actually bought
  • Clan bank interest is capped again, so a big treasury can no longer print coins overnight
Buffed instead
  • The free kit ladder (Rookie through Professor) had its item counts increased by about 50% — free kits pay in gear, not coins, so they got better instead of worse
What's staying the same
  • Competitive rewards are untouched — Gyms, the PvP Ladder and Dungeons pay exactly as before
  • Voting rewards and the Casino are unchanged

Every coin now carries more weight — thanks for building the economy with us. ❄️

🏪 The Bazaar & the Player Market

/shop/bazaar

Season 2 splits buying and selling into two places, on purpose. /shop is the Cobble Bazaar — the server's own store, with prices that move. /bazaar is a player-driven order book, and it's where things are actually worth money.

/shop — the Cobble Bazaar
  • The more an item is bought, the higher its buy price climbs
  • Selling into the shop relieves that pressure and pushes the price back down
  • Prices never fall below their base value, and buy always stays above sell — no infinite money printer
  • ⚠️ Shop buyback is only 2% of an item's buy price. The shop is a dump bin for junk, not a way to make money
/bazaar — the player order book

Selling to other players is the real market. The Bazaar works like a proper exchange:

  • Instant Buy / Instant Sell — take the best price on the book right now
  • Create a Buy Order or a Sell Offer — name your price and wait for someone to cross it
  • Every /shop item is tradeable, priced per single item, with a live buy/sell ladder you can read before committing
  • Orders are escrowed — your coins or items are held up front, so a fill is always honoured. Log off mid-order and your payout waits for you in Claim All
  • A small 1% tax comes out of seller proceeds and is burned — a permanent coin sink

Books start empty and players make the liquidity. Watch the ladder, undercut, sit on a bid, buy low and sell high — the Bazaar rewards paying attention to the economy instead of grinding one item forever. Alias: /baz.

Paradox Pokémon can no longer be caught from wild spawns. In Season 2 you craft them instead.

🐲 Raid Boss Revamp

Raid Bosses are moving out into the world — finding one is now part of the hunt.

Changes
  • Raid Bosses no longer spawn at a fixed location
  • They now spawn inside the Safari at random coordinates
  • No more automatic teleport to the boss
  • Players have to find it within the Safari zone
  • The Safari is now completely free to enter — no coin gate. With Raid Bosses, Dynamax/Gigantamax raids and Raid Dens all living out here, the Safari is open-access hunting ground for everyone
A raid's star rating is now its level

Every boss used to be Lv 100 no matter its tier, so the stars told you how long a raid would take but not how hard it would hit. Now the rating is the level:

★ Common 50  ·  ★★ Uncommon 60  ·  ★★★ Rare 70  ·  ★★★★ Epic 80  ·  ★★★★★ Legendary 90  ·  ★★★★★★ Mythic 100
  • A lower tier is both softer on offence and faster to chew through, so the low stars are now a genuine on-ramp
  • Mythic is exactly as it is today — nothing at the top got easier
  • Rewards, shared HP pools and timers are unchanged. Level is the only number that moved
Damage credit rewards damage, not stalling

Contribution decides who gets paid, and it could be gamed: padding a fight out with weak moves scored higher than a fast, focused knockout. Three separate faults all pushed that way and all three are fixed — a chip move no longer buys the type bonus, winning outright no longer hands you a free extra bar of credit, and dragging a fight past a few turns now costs you. A clean 2HKO is unchanged; a 12-turn stall no longer beats it.

⚡ Global Energy & Legend Revamp

/legend/legend roll

Legendary spawning moves from a shared server bar to something personal you build up yourself — and you decide when to cash it in.

Changes
  • /legend as the old shared system is scrapped, and Global Energy is now hidden
  • /legend is now per-player — everyone has their own Legend bar
  • The roll is manual. Nothing fires automatically: you pull the trigger yourself with /legend roll
  • After that roll, your Legend bar resets back to 0% — win or lose
  • Box legendaries are out of the wild pool — Kyurem, Rayquaza, Mewtwo, Zacian, Koraidon and the rest can't be rolled
A full bar is guaranteed — a partial one is a gamble

Your fill is your odds. Reach 100% and a Legendary will spawn for you — there is no chance roll left to lose. You no longer have to wait for 100% if you don't want to: from 20% up you can spend the bar early and gamble on it.

20% → 4%  ·  50% → 25%  ·  75% → 56%  ·  100% → guaranteed
  • The curve bends on purpose — an early spin is the worse bet, which is what makes waiting worth something
  • The whole bar is spent either way, win or lose, whatever it held. That is the price of impatience
  • Below 100% the wheel is built at your real odds, and a miss tells you exactly what you played
The roulette
  • Fate. An animated wheel at the odds your bar earned. A guaranteed spin skips this stage entirely — no suspense theatre when the answer is already yes
  • The Roster. The wheel spins across every Legendary eligible where you're standing, landing on the one that spawns for you
Filling your Legend bar
  • Complete Daily Quests and Weekly Quests
  • Win PvP battles
  • Defeat Alphas and Paradox Pokémon for Legendary energy
  • Minor Jobs activity still helps — but it's no longer the main driver
  • Catching, training, PokéHunts, Bingo and Battle Tower all still chip in

Every activity has a cap on how much it can contribute, so no single grind fills the bar — you reach 100% by playing across the server.

Where you stand matters

The roll respects the same spawn conditions /pwiki lists — biome, time of day and weather. If nothing is eligible where you're standing, your full bar is held, not spent: you'll be told to move somewhere a Legendary can actually appear, and you lose nothing.

🏆 Battle Tower Revamp

Battle Tower was fun initially, but eventually became repetitive and easy to cheese. Season 2 completely redesigns it into a roguelike climb.

Tower Changes
  • Removed all format-specific towers
  • Only Singles and Doubles remain
  • Opponents use randomized teams ranging from OU to AG
Survival Mechanics — It's a Nuzlocke
  • You draft 3 Pokémon from your party to start the run. The rest of your party is kept safe in your PC for the duration, and handed straight back when the run ends.
  • If a Pokémon goes down, it's out for the entire run. No revives — not from a heal, not from the Shop. Choose your leads carefully.
  • No healing between floors. HP, status and PP all carry from floor to floor, so you can't top up with /pokeheal, a potion or a healer — you have to bring a real team, not one OP move.
  • New team slots unlock at floors 10, 25 and 50 (up to 6). Each one lets you draft an extra Pokémon out of your PC from the /bt menu.
Opponent Scaling

  • 🔄 Swap — one switch: retire a downed Pokémon and bring a fresh one in from your PC, at full health. This is the only way to recover from a death.
  • 🛒 Open Shop — spend the Tokens you've earned climbing.

🏅 Gyms & Elite Four Revamp

Having 24 Elite Four members across multiple formats was simply too much. We're returning to a simpler progression.

Changes
  • One Elite Four
  • NatDex OU format
  • Requires 8 Gym Badges to challenge

Player Gym Leaders were a fun idea, but participation remained very low. Gym Badges will now play a much larger role in progression.

The ladder is the Kanto eight

The 18-type ladder is gone. Season 2 runs the Generation 1 roster — same eight types, same order, each awarding its canonical badge:

🪨 Rock · 💧 Water · ⚡ Electric · 🌿 Grass · ☠ Poison · ✨ Psychic · 🔥 Fire · ⛰ Ground
  • Challenge them in any order — there is no unlock chain
  • Four are Singles and four are Doubles, so no one team style clears the ladder
  • Every seat starts Season 2 vacant. Nobody carries a gym over from the beta — all eight are up for grabs
  • The roster follows the banner calendar: Gen 1 owns the opening month, and the ladder changes region as the calendar turns
Badges now gate your Rank

Badges are no longer just a trophy for the Elite Four — rank-ups require them:

Trainer 1 · Ace 2 · Ranger 3 · Veteran 4 · Elite 5 · Professor 6 · Master all 8

The count is lifetime, so the monthly gym reset can never un-earn a rank you already reached.

🥚 Breeding, Rebuilt From Scratch

/ascbreed/unbreed

Nobody liked the old breeding mod, so we wrote our own. Season 2 breeding is an in-house ASC system built around one idea borrowed from Pixelmon: where you breed matters. Building a good ranch is the investment, and the ranch is the sink.

Any pasture is a ranch
  • Place a Cobblemon pasture and right-click it — that's your ranch. No special block to craft or find
  • Assign two parents from your party; both appear beside the ranch and roam around inside it
  • You can run up to 3 active ranches at once
  • Check what you've got going with /ascbreed info
Build the right environment

A ranch reads the blocks around it and scores how well the environment suits your parents' types — faithful to the Pixelmon block table. Fire parents want campfires and magma; Ice parents want packed ice; Steel wants iron blocks; Dragon, Ghost and Psychic want emerald, and so on.

  • A block only counts if its type matches one of the parents — and remember Ditto counts as Normal
  • Only the highest block in each column counts, within a few blocks of the ranch's height
  • Environment strength runs 0 → 2 and directly sets your egg timer speed — a bad pen produces nothing
  • The ranch screen re-scans live, so you can watch strength climb as you place blocks
  • Range upgrades grow the pen from 9×9 up to 15×15 — and are refunded if the ranch is broken
Walk your eggs

Eggs are real items, and they hatch the mainline way: carry it and walk. Better IVs mean more steps, so a great egg makes you earn it. Hover the egg and it tells you exactly what's inside before it hatches — IVs, nature, ability, egg moves, size, shiny, and steps remaining.

Abilities that speed up hatching work as you'd expect: a parent with Flame Body, Magma Armor or Steam Engine halves the walk.

Full mainline inheritance
  • Destiny Knot — 5 inherited IVs · power items — pin a specific stat · Everstone — pass the nature (and regional form)
  • Masuda Method shiny odds, egg moves from the father or a benched parent, and Poké Ball inheritance
  • Size inheritance — babies can inherit a parent's scale, shown as a % on the egg
  • Light Ball on a parent → the bred Pichu knows Volt Tackle
  • Mirror Herb — teaches an egg move between incompatible parents with no egg produced
  • Special cases handled properly: Manaphy breeds Phione, Nidoran ♂/♀ and Volbeat/Illumise roll correctly, and baby forms (Munchlax, Wynaut, Happiny…) hatch as babies by default
Ditto × Ditto

The mainline forbids it. We don't. Two Dittos in a ranch produce a completely random species — a genuine gacha sink, with IVs, nature, size and ball still inherited from the pair. Legendaries and other restricted tiers are excluded.

Unbreedable Protection

Sellers can lock a Pokémon so buyers can't mass-breed it and crash its value.

  • Mark a Pokémon unbreedable with /unbreed — it will never produce eggs
  • Only the Original Trainer (or staff) can remove the lock
  • The lock travels with the Pokémon through trades, the GTS, gifts, and capsules — a bought battle-perfect Pokémon stays protected

Catch, then choose
  • Cast anywhere there's water. Each reel-in rolls a water or coastal species at a random level and size, filed into your Fish Bag
  • Left-click to claim — the Pokémon goes to your party or PC for real
  • Right-click to release — cash it in for coins instead

✦ Attributes & Item Gates

/profile/skills points/skills req

Five attributes sit on top of your skills — Strength, Dexterity, Vitality, Wisdom and Intellect. Each goes up to 20, each carries a passive, and some of the better gear won't work until the matching attribute is high enough. Open /profile to spend points and see exactly what you've unlocked.

Where attribute points come from

You earn points by levelling. They never drop, and they can't be bought. There is no crate, no quest and no rank that hands them out — two ladders you're already climbing pay them automatically, and they pay retroactively, so everything you levelled before the system existed already counted.

SourceRateHow you raise it
Power Level1 point per 25Your 15 skill levels added up. Every ore, log, catch, battle and hatched egg pushes it — /skills shows the lot.
Account Level1 point per 3Your /level. Each Prestige Star counts as 50 levels, so prestiging never costs you points.
  • Both ladders pay at the same time — they're added, not picked between
  • 100 points is the lifetime cap, which is exactly enough to max all five attributes
  • /skills points tells you your current numbers and how many more skill levels or account levels until the next point
  • Changed your mind? Respec refunds every spent point for 25,000 coins

Worked example: Power Level 137 and account level 8 → 137 ÷ 25 = 5 points, plus 8 ÷ 3 = 2 points, for 7 earned. Thirteen more skill levels, or one more account level, buys the next one.

How the gates actually work
  • Use is gated, not ownership. You can craft, trade, carry and store anything — a gated item simply won't swing, mine, place or stay equipped until you qualify
  • Wood, stone, gold and iron gear is free — tools and armour are only gated at diamond and netherite, so nothing you make in your first hours is locked
  • Nothing Pokémon-related is ever gated — Poké Balls, TMs, Mega Showdown items, ASC crate keys and Forge relic gear all bypass the system completely
  • Holding a gated item never stops you opening doors, chests or anything else
  • /skills req tells you what the item in your hand needs, and whether you clear it
