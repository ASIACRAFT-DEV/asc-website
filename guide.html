Season 2 Gameplay Overhaul

Every activity now has a purpose. Progress by actually playing the game — not just farming coins.

⚠️ Disclaimer: These changes are not final and are subject to change before Season 2 launches.

Season 2 is all about giving every activity a purpose. In Season 1, we noticed a few unhealthy gameplay loops:

  • Farming money became the best strategy for almost everything.
  • Legendary Pokémon became less valuable because anyone could eventually gamble for them.
  • Battle Pass rewards weren't exciting enough.
  • Battle Tower became repetitive.
  • Some progression systems had too many requirements.

Season 2 aims to fix these problems.

🎰 Banner & Summon Revamp

/gamble/ascsummon/banner

One of the biggest problems we saw was that players would simply grind money and gamble until they obtained every Legendary. This made trading less meaningful and removed the excitement of obtaining rare Pokémon.

Changes
  • Pity increased from 90 → 250 pulls
  • Banners now use Banner Pulls, a special currency — mainly earned through gameplay
  • You can still buy a limited 10–20 pulls per day with coins, and coin gains are being heavily nerfed
You can obtain Banner Pulls through
  • Battle Tower
  • Area Zero
  • Dungeons
  • Daily Quests
  • Weekly Quests
  • Battle Pass
  • Banner Rotation

Banners are now divided into Versions, each lasting 4 weeks. Instead of Trio Banners, we'll now feature Generation Banners (Gen 1–9).

This keeps banners fresh while making Legendary Pokémon more valuable again.

💰 Coins & Economy Revamp

Money is no longer the driving force for Legendaries — but coins still matter. In Season 2 they shift from "buy everything" to fuelling the content loop.

What coins are now for
  • Shop purchases, as always
  • Safari entrance fee — Raid Bosses now spawn inside the Safari (no more fixed Spawn location, and no forced teleport to bosses)
  • Entry cost for the content that rewards Banner Pulls — Battle Tower, Area Zero, Dungeons and Raid Bosses
  • Dungeon Keys are now purchasable with coins — the daily key allowance is removed
  • A limited 10–20 Banner Pulls per day can still be bought with coins
  • Buy Claim Blocks and extra PC slots
  • Convert coins directly into Tokens and Shards

Coin gains across the server are being heavily nerfed, so every coin carries more weight.

🏪 Cobble Bazaar — Dynamic Shop

/shop

Season 2's /shop becomes the Cobble Bazaar — a living market where prices move with supply and demand instead of sitting at fixed values.

How prices move
  • The more an item is bought, the higher its buy price climbs
  • The more an item is sold, the lower its sell price drops
  • Buy price always stays above sell price — no infinite money printer
  • Prices slowly heal back toward baseline over time

Watch the market, buy low and sell high — the Bazaar rewards paying attention to the economy instead of grinding one item forever.

🧰 Jobs Revamp

/jobs

Jobs move from a passive income system to an activity-driven questline. Instead of coins trickling in while you grind one action, you complete job quests to earn Paychecks — then claim them from that job's dedicated NPC.

The new loop
  • Do job activity → complete a quest → earn a Paycheck + Job EXP + Reputation
  • Visit the Job NPC to accept quests, track progress, and claim your paycheck
  • Paychecks can't be claimed remotely — no AFK salary, no automation

Rewards include
  • Banner Pulls every 10 levels
  • Premium Pass receives double Banner Pull rewards
  • Seasonal Essence
  • IV Modifiers
  • SBCs / GBCs
  • Various progression materials

🌌 Area Zero Revamp

Area Zero has consistently been our most popular endgame activity, so we're expanding it significantly.

New Features
  • PvP-enabled zones with 2x loot
  • Alpha, Paradox, and Legendary Pokémon can spawn inside PvP zones
  • Only Alphas can be caught — catch privilege is determined by Last Hit
  • Paradox aren't catchable here — defeat them for Elemental DNA to use in Paradox Crafting
  • Chance to obtain: Banner Pulls, Elemental DNA, IV Modifiers
Spawn System
  • Alpha every 10 minutes
  • Paradox every 30 minutes
  • After defeating 5 Alphas + 5 Paradox Pokémon, a Legendary will spawn

Progress is now visible directly on the Area Zero HUD.

🧬 Paradox Crafting

Paradox Pokémon can no longer be caught from wild spawns. In Season 2 you craft them instead.

Base Pokémon + Elemental DNA → evolves into its Paradox form

Elemental DNA is earned from endgame content like Area Zero. This makes every Paradox a deliberate goal you work toward — not a lucky wild encounter.

🐲 Raid Boss Revamp

Raid Bosses are moving out into the world — finding one is now part of the hunt.

Changes
  • Raid Bosses no longer spawn at a fixed location
  • They now spawn inside the Safari at random coordinates
  • No more automatic teleport to the boss
  • Players have to find it within the Safari zone

📒 Pokédex Research Quests

Inspired by Legends: Arceus, every Pokémon gets its own research quest — giving the Safari a real purpose beyond just catching, and giving players something to do other than dungeon speedruns and fishing.

How it works
  • Every Pokémon across Gen 1–9 — and each stage of its evolution line — has its own quest
  • Tasks include catching a species, using specific moves, evolving, and leveling it up a set number of times
  • No quests for Legendaries, since they don't spawn naturally in the Safari
Example — Charmander line
Charmander
Catch Charmander ×10 · Use Tackle ×5 · Use Ember ×5 · Use Flamethrower ×5 · Evolve to Charmeleon ×3 · Level up Charmander ×25
Rewards
  • Coins (e.g. ~50k) plus materials like Rare Candies per quest
  • Final-evolution quests pay out more — e.g. a Charizard quest for 100k + a Master Ball

Exact reward values are still being decided.

⚡ Global Energy & Legend Revamp

Legendary spawning moves from a shared server bar to something personal you build up yourself.

Changes
  • /legend as the old shared system is scrapped, and Global Energy is now hidden
  • /legend is now per-player — everyone has their own Legend bar
  • When your Legend bar is full, there's a 25% chance a Legendary spawns for you — if you meet the biome requirements
  • After that roll, your Legend chance resets back to 0%
Filling your Legend bar
  • Complete Daily Quests and Weekly Quests
  • Win PvP battles
  • Defeat Alphas and Paradox Pokémon for Legendary energy
  • Minor Jobs activity still helps — but it's no longer the main driver

Every activity has a cap on how much it can contribute, so no single grind fills the bar — you reach 100% by playing across the server.

🏆 Battle Tower Revamp

Battle Tower was fun initially, but eventually became repetitive and easy to cheese. Season 2 completely redesigns it into a roguelike climb.

Tower Changes
  • Removed all format-specific towers
  • Only Singles and Doubles remain
  • Opponents use randomized teams ranging from OU to AG
Survival Mechanics — It's a Nuzlocke
  • You draft 3 Pokémon from your party to start the run. The rest of your party is kept safe in your PC for the duration, and handed straight back when the run ends.
  • If a Pokémon goes down, it's out for the entire run. No revives — not from a heal, not from the Shop. Choose your leads carefully.
  • No healing between floors. HP, status and PP all carry from floor to floor, so you can't top up with /pokeheal, a potion or a healer — you have to bring a real team, not one OP move.
  • New team slots unlock at floors 10, 25 and 50 (up to 6). Each one lets you draft an extra Pokémon out of your PC from the /bts2 menu.
Opponent Scaling

A fresh 3-mon draft doesn't open against a full team of 6 — the tower ramps up with you:

Checkpoints — Choose One

Every 10 floors you hit a checkpoint and pick exactly one reward. Your choice is saved — closing the menu or logging out won't waste it, just reopen /bts2.

  • ❤ Heal Party — restore HP, status and PP on your surviving Pokémon. Does not revive downed Pokémon.
  • 🔄 Swap — one switch: retire a downed Pokémon and bring a fresh one in from your PC, at full health. This is the only way to recover from a death.
  • 🛒 Open Shop — spend the BT Tokens you've earned climbing.
Quality of Life
  • Checkpoints are now saved every floor
  • Disconnecting no longer sends you back several floors
Balance Changes
  • Fusions are allowed in the Tower — and NPCs now use fusion Pokémon too
  • Cosmog Ray nerfed — its trivializing one-shot spam is toned down, so AG isn't the only climb worth doing
  • Sorry, Cosmog Ray and Gardevoir Queen enjoyers 😅

🏅 Gyms & Elite Four Revamp

Having 24 Elite Four members across multiple formats was simply too much. We're returning to a simpler progression.

Changes
  • One Elite Four
  • NatDex OU format
  • Requires 8 Gym Badges to challenge

Player Gym Leaders were a fun idea, but participation remained very low. Gym Badges will now play a much larger role in progression.

🛡️ Clan Revamp

Clans get a full progression and territory system in Season 2 — building a clan base is now something you earn and invest in.

Clan Permit System

Setting up a clan base now follows clear steps:

  • Obtain a Clan Permit
  • Create your clan
  • Buy a Clan Base Build Permit to claim territory
Base Sizes

Build permits come in three territory sizes:

  • 250 × 250
  • 500 × 500
  • 1000 × 1000

The claimed area becomes clan territory, which clan officers can expand.

Official Clans & Land Tax
  • Official clans are recognized on the server
  • They pay a 5% land tax, scaled to how large their territory is
Membership & Alliances
  • Clans are capped at 5 members — you can buy up to +5 extra slots
  • The cap keeps players from merging into one mega-clan
  • Set relations with /clan ally and /clan enemy
Social & Clan NPC
  • Clan chat and ally chat
  • A Clan NPC auto-spawns at the center of the clan build, or the leader can place it
Clan Raid Boss
  • Each clan gets its own dedicated Raid Boss
  • It spawns on a fixed timer
  • Only that clan's members can hit it
Clan Wars (replaces Warzone)

The old Warzone is being removed and rolled into Clan Wars — proper clan-vs-clan conflict.

  • Clan Raid Boss spawns and clan Pokémon battles
  • When a trainer loses a battle, they forfeit 1 Pokémon to the enemy clan
  • After the war match, the losing clan can buy back its stolen Pokémon
  • If they don't buy them back, the winning clan keeps them as War Tokens

⚔ PvP Ranked Revamp

/pvp

Since PvP is now part of rank progression, we're redesigning Ranked as well.

Ranked Tiers
  • Poké Ball IV → I
  • Great Ball IV → I
  • Ultra Ball IV → I
  • Master Tier
Master Tier

Only 10 players may hold Master Tier at any given time. To qualify:

  • Reach Ultra Ball I
  • Rankings are based on total wins
  • Every 24 hours, the Top 10 eligible players are promoted into Master Tier

Master Tier is designed to be competitive, prestigious, and constantly changing.

🥚 Breeding Changes

/unbreed

Breeding gets fairer in Season 2 — genetics behave the way they should, and the Pokémon market stays healthy.

Hidden Abilities
  • An egg can only hatch with a Hidden Ability if a parent actually has one
  • No more Hidden Abilities appearing out of nowhere when neither parent had one
  • Ditto doesn't pass its own Hidden Ability — same as the official games
  • Normal ability inheritance is unchanged, so genuine HA breeding works exactly as before
Unbreedable Protection

Sellers can lock a Pokémon so buyers can't mass-breed it and crash its value.

  • Mark a Pokémon unbreedable with /unbreed — it will never produce eggs
  • Only the Original Trainer (or staff) can remove the lock
  • The lock travels with the Pokémon through trades, the GTS, gifts, and capsules — a bought battle-perfect Pokémon stays protected

Final Thoughts

Season 2 is focused on making every activity matter. Instead of farming one thing for everything else, you'll progress by actually playing the game:

  • 🏆 Climb Battle Tower for Banner Pulls.
  • 🌌 Explore Area Zero for rare resources.
  • ⚔ Compete in PvP to reach Master Tier.
  • 🏅 Challenge Gyms and the Elite Four.
  • 📖 Complete the Battle Pass.
  • 🎰 Spend your hard-earned Banner Pulls on the current banner rotation.

Every system is now connected, creating a more rewarding endgame where time spent playing translates directly into progression — not just more coins.
