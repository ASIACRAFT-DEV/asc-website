Cobble Asia rules

By joining the server you agree to these. Anything not covered here is up to staff, and staff decisions are final (appeal by opening a ticket on Discord).

In-game rules

  1. 1. No cheating, exploiting or duping.Hacked clients, macros and auto-clickers are not allowed.
  2. 2. No griefing or stealingfrom other players.
  3. 3. Be respectful in chat.No slurs, harassment or spam.
  4. 4. English in main chat.Use clan or party chat for other languages.
  5. 5. Advertising other servers = permanent ban.
  6. 6. One account per player.Alt accounts are banned on sight. Sharing a connection with someone, or using ExitLag? Open a ticket before a second account joins.
  7. 7. Poké Snacks:at most 4 within 16 blocks. No snack towers.
  8. 8. No dex trading.Trading a Pokémon away and getting it back, or swapping a cheap Pokémon for a legendary with no payment, to fill your dex.
  9. 9. Area Zero is full PvP.Items lost there are not compensated.
  10. 10. Staff decisions are final.To appeal, open a ticket on Discord instead of arguing in chat.
  11. 11. Found a bug or exploit? Report it right awayin the Discord bug-reports forum or a ticket. Abusing an exploit = permanent ban.
  12. 12. No impersonation.Do not impersonate staff members, owners, other players, bots or official server representatives. This includes using misleading usernames, nicknames, skins or messages to deceive others.
  13. 13. No scamming or deceptive trading.Players are free to negotiate their own prices, and knowingly overpaying for a Pokémon or item is entirely their choice. However, deliberately taking advantage of another player's lack of knowledge about an item's or Pokémon's value, while knowingly concealing the actual value or misleading them, is scamming. This also includes misrepresenting a Pokémon's stats, IVs, EVs, nature, ability, rarity or other relevant attributes, and accepting payment without delivering the agreed assets. A difference in market value alone is not scamming.
  14. 14. No harassment.Harassment through private or public messages is prohibited.
  15. 15. No evading punishments.Do not use another account, another player's account or any other method to get around an active mute, jail sentence, temporary ban or other staff restriction.
  16. 16. No inappropriate usernames, Pokémon names or skins.Usernames, Pokémon nicknames, skins and anything else other players can see must not contain offensive, discriminatory, sexually explicit or otherwise inappropriate content.
  17. 17. No event or tournament manipulation.Do not manipulate official server events, tournaments, leaderboards or competitions through coordinated cheating, fabricated results, match manipulation or deliberately getting around event rules.
  18. 18. No excessive spam or chat flooding.Do not flood public chat with repeated messages, excessive character repetition, unnecessary command spam or disruptive content. This also covers repeated begging and deliberately derailing conversations.
  19. 19. No misleading staff or fabricated reports.Do not deliberately submit false reports, fabricate evidence, edit screenshots or give misleading information during a staff investigation. A good-faith report that turns out to be wrong is not punished.
  20. 20. No threatening or coercing other players.Do not threaten, blackmail, extort or pressure players into handing over Pokémon, items, currency, accounts or anything else. This includes threatening fake reports or using "staff connections" to intimidate others.
Also counts as exploiting (rule 1): arranging Warzone losses with another player (win-trading). The first time is a warning; doing it again means jail.

Enforcement

A first-time violation will generally get a warning. Further action is at staff discretion, depending on how serious the violation was, whether it was intentional and whether it has happened before. Severe violations may get immediate action.

Discord

The Discord server has its own rules in the rules channel: use the right channel, be patient, no advertising, your nickname must be your in-game name, respect everyone, no bullying, no spam, no NSFW, no impersonating users, staff or bots, and tell staff if something feels wrong.

Reports and appeals

Open a ticket on Discord to report a player or appeal a decision. Bugs go in the bug-reports forum.
